\section*{Artificial Intelligence Utilization Statement}

In adherence to rigorous standards of transparency and methodological reproducibility, we hereby disclose the utilize of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools in the development of the \textit{Sysmic} framework and the preparation of associated manuscripts. The following Large Language Models (LLMs) were employed to accelerate prototyping, scaffold code architectures, and structure intermediate analytical outputs. The systems are listed in descending order of operational intensity and relevance to the project:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Antigravity} (powered by Gemini 3 Pro)
    \item \textbf{DeepSeek v3.2}
    \item \textbf{Claude 4.5 Sonnet}
    \item \textbf{Google NotebookLM}
    \item \textbf{Qwen 3 Max}
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Critical Assessment of AI Contribution}

While these computational agents provided undeniable utility in articulating initial drafts and vertebrating complex software modules—thereby accelerating the production timeline significantly—their net contribution was demarcated by severe operational limitations.

\subsection*{Data Integrity and Hallucination}
Despite explicit, redundant, and severe instructional constraints regarding data sanctity, the employed models exhibited a persistent stochastic tendency towards ``hallucination'' and the fabrication of fictitious references or data points. Consequently, the tools proved conducive to scientific workflow only under a regime of \textbf{constant, line-by-line supervision}. Every segment of generated code, translation, or academic prose required exhaustive human verification to ensure alignment with physical reality and mathematical rigor.

\subsection*{Contextual and Mnemonical Degradation}
Furthermore, contrary to the architectural specifications advertised by model providers, we observed varying degrees of effective context window collapse and a progressive, exasperating degradation of long-term memory within interaction sessions. This phenomenon often necessitated the iterative re-injection of core project constraints, diminishing the theoretical efficiency gains.

\section*{Conclusion}
Our experience delineates a sharp frontier between the \textit{expected} net contribution of current AI agents and the \textit{genuine} utility they offer in a high-precision scientific context. While valuable as accelerators for scaffolding and formatting (`maquetación`), their capacity to function autonomously without deviating into invention remains, at present, insufficient for rigorous scientific inquiry without pervasive human oversight.
